# G.In: Startup Co-Pilot & Stress-Test

**A frank, step-by-step co-pilot that stress-tests a startup idea before you build it.**

G.In helps early-stage founders, especially students, test an idea against real-world risks and find cheap ways to validate it first. It gives honest verdicts instead of flattery, one step at a time. It was created for the **Guaranteed.In** idea (an advisor-backed founder platform), and the same approach works for any idea.

> ⚠️ **Not legal or financial advice.** Legal references in the app (for example Indian Contract Act or company law points) are guidance for further research. Verify them with a qualified lawyer or company secretary before acting.

## What it does

- **Risk Stress-Test:** Breaks the idea into the top 7 risks, each rated Fatal, High, or Medium, with an India-specific reality check.
- **Cheap validation tests:** Every risk comes with a ₹0 test (a DM, a short interview, a Google Form) and a time estimate.
- **Brutal verdict:** A clear call: proceed, narrow, or pivot, with a recommended direction.
- **24-Hour Action Plan:** One concrete next step to take within a day.
- **Budget and studies reality check:** Keeps the plan realistic around a tiny budget and academic commitments.
- **10-Step Roadmap:** Moves from stress-test to competitor research, business model, validation plan, legal outline, trust and safety, MVP spec, go-to-market, metrics, and funding.
- **Status tracking:** Mark each risk as untested, tested, or resolved as you collect real answers.
- **Field notes:** Log advisor and user feedback. Notes auto-save in your browser's local storage.
- **Parked ideas:** Unclear concepts are held aside until the core idea is proven.

## Example output (Step 1)

- **Verdict:** Do not proceed as currently designed. Narrow and pivot.
- **Suggested pivot:** Micro-advisory milestones and peer vouching.
- **Sample risks:** the "guarantee" liability trap, equity-only incentive problems, the black-box vs vouching paradox, marketplace cold start, the shared-responsibility illusion, equity legality for unincorporated ideas, and platform disintermediation.

## How it works

1. Open the app and review the Step 1 verdict.
2. Run the cheap validation test on each risk.
3. Record what you learn in Field Notes and update each risk's status.
4. Take the 24-hour action, then move to the next step.

## Privacy

All notes and statuses stay in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server by the notes feature. Clear your browser data to remove them.
